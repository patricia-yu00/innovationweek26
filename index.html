    Why we built this

    Making a village of agents legible

    Read your agents the way you read a room full of people: at a glance, with no parsing and no rebuilding context.

    01

    The problem

    More agents, more to keep track of

    As more agents run across CXO products, keeping track of them adds to marketers' workload.

    • 📡Missed signalsImportant updates get buried
    • 🐢Slow responsesHandoffs stall while nobody's watching
    • 🤝Lost trustIt's hard to trust what you can't see
    • 🧠Cognitive overloadToo many feeds, logs and badges

    Our goal: make multi-agent orchestration visually legible, so marketers can manage their agents through spatial awareness.

    02

    Founding hypothesis

    Our bet, in five steps

    1. If we help

      Marketing practitioners who orchestrate agentic workflows across Experience Cloud

    2. solve

      The inability to see what multiple agents are doing

    3. with

      A character-driven spatial metaphor that shows each agent's role, state, autonomy and relationships

    4. they'll choose it over

      Dashboards, activity feeds, notification badges and status logs

    5. because it's

      Readable at a glance, like a room full of people

    03

    What makes it different

    From one character to a whole cast

    Today

    Single-agent characters

    Meta and OpenAI each found that giving an agent a character builds trust, but both built it for one agent at a time.

    →
    charmz

    A multi-agent ensemble

    The relationships between characters show how the agents coordinate. That's exactly where Adobe's workflows fit in.
